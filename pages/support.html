Inflation Tracker Support

Write to us: trackerinflation@gmail.com

Tell us what happened, your device and the app's version (Settings ▸ Version), and we can help faster.

To tell us what to fix without an answer, use Settings ▸ Enjoying Inflation Tracker? ▸ Not happy? Send us feedback in the app. It sends your note with the app's version, so you don't need to add it.

My data doesn't sync between my devices.

Your devices need to be signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, with iCloud allowed for the app. Settings ▸ iCloud Sync shows the sync status. The first sync can take a few minutes.

I bought Pro but another device doesn't show it.

Tap Settings ▸ Restore Purchases. A purchase works on every iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch that uses the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, go to Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Inflation Tracker. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends and it won't renew. The lifetime purchase is one-time and never renews.

How do I remove the sample data?

Tap the sample data card on Overview, or use Settings ▸ Remove Sample Data. Only the samples are removed. Whatever you added stays.

How do I add widgets?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit ▸ Add Widget and choose Inflation Tracker. Settings ▸ Widgets & Shortcuts shows every widget with a preview, and you can add them to Overview from there too.

Can my card payments be logged automatically?

Yes. Add the ready-made shortcut from Settings ▸ Widgets & Shortcuts ▸ Shortcuts. With a “Transaction” automation set up in the Shortcuts app, each card payment is logged as an expense with its amount and merchant. The category comes from what you chose at that merchant before, or from its name.

Can I import a bank statement or another app's data?

Yes. Import CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files from Tracking ▸ Files (on the Mac, Files in the sidebar). You choose which column fills which field, nothing is saved until you confirm, and every import can be undone. Export is a Pro feature.

How is my personal inflation calculated?

When you track an item, its unit price is saved each time you buy it. Every month, the app compares what you bought with its previous price and weights the changes by how much you spend on each item. Official rates are in Settings ▸ Rates, and their sources in Settings ▸ Data Sources.

I don't see the assistant.

The assistant appears on devices that support Apple Intelligence and run iOS 26 or macOS 26, while Apple Intelligence is on.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings ▸ Delete All Data removes the records on every device signed in to the same iCloud account. It can't be undone.
